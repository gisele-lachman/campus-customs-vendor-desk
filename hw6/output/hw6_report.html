5Recommendation to the store manager

Yes, turn it on, as a reading assistant, not a buyer. With all three defenses it got all 12 practice documents right, and both of my red-team attacks once the attachment gap was patched. It never tried to email or pay anyone, and it costs well under a cent per document. Keep a person in charge of every decision that spends money.

What it would cost

Real cost per exam document (full defense): $0.001586 ÷ 10 = $0.000159
1,000 documents a day × 30 days = 30,000 documents
30,000 × $0.000159 = ≈ $4.76 a month
Worst case, if the cache never helped: 30,000 × $0.001388 (a real cache-missed run) ≈ $41.63 a month. For comparison, no defense: 30,000 × $0.000318 ≈ $9.53 a month.

Assumptions: prices from data/prices.json (input $0.10, cached $0.01, cache write $0.125, output $0.50 per million tokens); documents similar in size to the exam set; one model call per document; the cache stays warm through the day; Chrome time and a person's review time are not counted.

Limits to know

  • Without the defenses the model makes comparison mistakes: on clean p08 it said $4.27 was above the $5.34 limit. Keep the full defense on; it makes the decision in code.
  • It needs Google Chrome on the machine to clean HTML. If Chrome is missing it stops rather than passing uncleaned text. Chrome once hung under heavy parallel load (the run failed safely and passed on retry).
  • A quote that comes only as an email attachment is now rejected, because attachments are dropped. Those need a person.
  • Text a person can see is passed through on purpose; a visible contradictory “update” can still push a quote toward rejection.

Rules for using it

  • A human signs off before any email or payment. The agent can't send or pay anything today; keep it that way.
  • A person looks at every document where hidden text was removed, especially when the agent still says recommend (e03_quote.html, e06_quote.pdf, e10_email.eml in the exam set).
  • Recheck a sample of decisions each week against the handbook, and re-run the red-team files after any change.
